Programme Options

Pathway Programmes

Choose the pathway that fits your timeline. All programmes deliver 1200 learning hours and are aligned to the UK RQF Level 3 — earning you 120 transferable credits.

Standard

Standard Pathway

9 Months · Balanced Pace

A structured, balanced 9-month route offering steady academic preparation and a smooth transition into international higher education. Ideal for students who have completed Class 12 and want thorough preparation before heading abroad.

  • Structured 9-month curriculum (1200 learning hours)
  • 120 transferable credit hours across four core subjects
  • Level 3 Diploma in International Pathway Studies on completion
  • Guaranteed progression to partner universities
  • Direct entry into Year 2 of 4-year undergraduate degrees
  • Strong English for Academic Purposes (EAP) component
  • Accommodation available: ₹1.5 Lakhs
Accelerated

Accelerated Pathway

6 Months · Fast-Track

An intensive 6-month programme designed for fast-track progression — ideal for students targeting September university intake. Covers the same 1200 learning hours in a condensed, focused format with dedicated academic support.

  • Intensive 6-month fast-track format
  • Ideal for September university entry abroad
  • Same 120 credit hours as Standard Pathway
  • Level 3 Diploma in International Pathway Studies
  • Guaranteed progression to partner universities
  • High-intensity academic and language support
  • Accommodation available: ₹1.2 Lakhs
Also available: Integrated Pathway (up to 18 months) for Class 11 students or those studying alongside other commitments. | International Year One (IYO) — 1-year, 2-semester programme with direct progression to Year 2. Details on request.

A Pathway Programme is a structured preparatory programme that bridges the gap between your current qualifications and the entry requirements of international universities. Unlike applying directly, it helps you:
  • Qualify even if you don't meet direct entry requirements
  • Drastically lower your Year 1 tuition and living expenses by studying in India
  • Earn 120 transferable credits and progress into Year 2 of a 4-year degree abroad
  • Build the academic and language foundation needed to thrive internationally
